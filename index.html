TF2 Voice Emulator

Make any audio or video sound like it came through Team Fortress 2 voice chat.

  • Real Opus 24 kHz · VBR + DTX · SILK/CELT hybrid
  • Receiver auto-gain with the in-game int16 clip
  • Measured against real TF2 recordings
  • Video in, video out MP4 · MOV, picture untouched
  • Local — nothing is uploaded
